#+STARTUP: content
* referer origin yt-dlp
** browser

1) open the browser developer tools by pressing F12
2) open the network inspector
3) play the video
4) find and copy the playlist url which may not have a m3u8 extension, it may be called x-mpegurl
5) Right Click on the .m3u8 request or one of the video segment requests (.ts or .mp4 files).
6) in the contextual menu select copy and then copy value, copy request headers
7) paste into a text file
8) look for the origin and referer

#+begin_example
Origin: https://example.com
Referer: https://example.com/
#+end_example
   
** yt-dlp command

add the referer, origin and playlist url below

#+begin_src sh
yt-dlp --referer "https://example.com/" --add-header "Origin: https://example.com/" "https://example.com/playlist.m3u8" -o example.mkv
#+end_src
